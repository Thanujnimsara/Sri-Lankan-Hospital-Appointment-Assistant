Emergency Services: 1990 Suwa Seriya Ambulance Hotline:
- Overview:
  - 1990 Suwa Seriya is Sri Lanka's free national Pre-Hospital Care Ambulance Service established by government act.
  - Toll-free emergency hotline: 1990 (Accessible from any landline or mobile network 24/7/365).
- Services Provided:
  - Free emergency dispatch of medical ambulances equipped with basic life support (BLS), defibrillators, oxygen, and trained Emergency Medical Technicians (EMTs).
  - Rapid transportation of acute medical/trauma patients to the nearest government or private hospital.
- Call Guidelines:
  1. Dial 1990 immediately for medical emergencies (heart attack, stroke, major accidents, severe breathing difficulty, unconsciousness).
  2. State your location, nearest landmark, patient condition, and contact phone number clearly.
