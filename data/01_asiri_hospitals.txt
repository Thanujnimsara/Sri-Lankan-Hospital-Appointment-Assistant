Asiri Hospital Group Overview & Channelling Guide:
- Main Locations:
  1. Asiri Central Hospital: No. 114, Norris Canal Road, Colombo 10. Phone: +94 11 452 4400.
  2. Asiri Surgical Hospital: No. 21, Kirimandala Mawatha, Colombo 05. Phone: +94 11 452 4400.
  3. Asiri Hospital Matara & Kandy branches.
- Channelling Services:
  - Doctor channelling fee ranges from LKR 2,000 to LKR 4,500 depending on the consultant.
  - Hospital booking fee: LKR 650 - LKR 950 per appointment.
  - Walk-in OPD is open 24/7 at both Central and Surgical units.
  - Specialist appointment slots can be reserved via eChannelling, Doc990, or direct hotline +94 11 452 4400.
- Emergency & Facilities:
  - 24-Hour Emergency Care Unit, ICU, Cardiac Care Unit, Radiology, and Laboratory Services.
