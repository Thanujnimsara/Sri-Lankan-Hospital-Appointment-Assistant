Doc990 Booking & Refund FAQ:
- What is Doc990?
  - Doc990 is a leading digital doctor channelling platform in Sri Lanka powered by Dialog Axiata in partnership with major private hospital groups.
- How to Book via Doc990:
  1. Dial 990 from any Dialog, Mobitel, Hutch, or Airtel mobile.
  2. Or visit doc990.lk / download the Doc990 Mobile App.
  3. Search for Doctor / Specialty / Hospital and pick an available session.
  4. Confirm patient details and pay via Dialog Add-to-Bill, Genie, Credit Card, or Star Points.
- Cancellation & Refund Policy:
  - Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled doctor session start time.
  - Doctor fees are non-refundable if cancelled on the day of appointment unless the doctor cancels/postpones the session.
  - Service charges are non-refundable.
  - Hotline for refund requests: Call 990 (Dialog) or +94 11 799 0990.
