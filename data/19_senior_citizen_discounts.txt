Senior Citizen Discounts & Privilege Card Healthcare Benefits:
- Senior Citizen Healthcare Benefits (Age 60+):
  - Special discounts on hospital service charges, laboratory investigations, and wellness packages at major private hospitals (Asiri, Nawaloka, Lanka Hospitals, Durdans, Hemas).
  - Discount range: 5% to 15% discount on OPD laboratory tests and radiology (X-Ray, Ultrasound, CT/MRI).
- How to Claim Discounts:
  - Present valid Sri Lankan National Identity Card (NIC) showing age over 60 at the registration or payment counter.
  - Present hospital membership / privilege card (e.g. Asiri Amazing Care, Nawaloka Senior Gold, Lanka Hospitals Silver Card) during channelling token issuance.
