Accepted Payment Methods for Channelling in Sri Lanka:
- Mobile Network Add-to-Bill:
  - Dialog: Add booking charges directly to your postpaid monthly bill or deduct from prepaid balance via 990 or Doc990.lk.
  - Mobitel / SLT: Add booking fee to Mobitel monthly bill via 225 or eChannelling.
- Digital Wallets & Mobile Money:
  - eZ Cash: Pay directly using your eZ Cash mobile wallet account on Dialog, Hutch, or Etisalat lines.
  - mCash: Supported on SLT-Mobitel platforms.
  - LankaPay QR / Genie / Frimi / FLASH app scanning at checkout.
- Bank Payment Cards:
  - Visa, MasterCard, UnionPay, and Amex credit/debit cards issued by any Sri Lankan or international bank.
- Cash Payments:
  - Pay cash directly at hospital channelling counters or designated islandwide partner outlets (Cargills Food City, Singer outlets, Abans, Telecommunication agent shops).
