National Hospital Sri Lanka (NHSL) & Public Healthcare Services:
- Location: EW Perera Mawatha, Colombo 10. Phone: +94 11 269 1111.
- Public OPD Hours:
  - Morning OPD: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM.
  - Afternoon OPD: 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM.
  - Sunday OPD: Limited emergency services only.
- Referral & Specialist Clinics:
  - Public hospital consultant clinics require a valid referral letter from an OPD doctor or a government Divisional Hospital.
  - All treatment, consultations, standard diagnostic tests, and basic medications at government hospitals like NHSL are 100% free of charge for Sri Lankan citizens.
- Emergency Services:
  - NHSL Accident & Emergency Unit (AEU) is open 24/7 for acute trauma and emergencies.
