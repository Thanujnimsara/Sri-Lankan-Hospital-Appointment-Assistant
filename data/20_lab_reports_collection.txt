Laboratory Reports Online Retrieval & Collection Procedures:
- Online Lab Report Access:
  - Major hospital chains (Asiri, Lanka Hospitals, Nawaloka, Durdans, Hemas) provide digital lab report downloads via their official websites or mobile apps.
  - Required details for online download: Bill Receipt Number / Lab Reference Number and Patient Mobile Number or PIN printed on the receipt.
- Physical Collection:
  - Reports can be collected at 24/7 Laboratory Counters or regional sample collection centers.
  - Patient or authorized representative must present the original payment receipt or valid ID.
- Fasting & Preparation Reminders:
  - Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) & Lipid Profile: Fasting for 8 - 12 hours required before blood draw. Water is permitted.
