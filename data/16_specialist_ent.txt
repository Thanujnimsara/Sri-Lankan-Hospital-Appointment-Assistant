Specialist Guide: Consultant ENT Surgeon (Ear, Nose & Throat):
- When to See an ENT Specialist (Otolaryngologist):
  - Ear infections, hearing loss, tinnitus (ringing in ears), vertigo/dizziness, chronic sinusitis, nasal obstruction, tonsillitis, persistent sore throat, voice hoarseness, snoring, and sleep apnea.
- Common Procedures:
  - Nasal endoscopy, ear wax microsuction, audiometry (hearing test) coordination, tonsillectomy, and sinus surgery.
- Tips for Visit:
  - Mention any exposure to loud noise, recent air travel, or chronic allergy history.
