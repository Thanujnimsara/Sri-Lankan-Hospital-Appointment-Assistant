Specialist Guide: Consultant Neurologist & Neuro-surgeon:
- When to See a Neurologist:
  - Chronic severe headaches, migraines, numbness or tingling sensations in limbs, seizures/epilepsy, memory loss, balance disorders, tremors, or weakness in limbs.
- Neuro-surgeon vs Neurologist:
  - Neurologist: Non-surgical diagnosis and medical treatment of brain, spinal cord, and nerve disorders.
  - Neuro-surgeon: Surgical treatment of brain tumors, spinal disc prolapse, stroke hematomas, and severe head trauma.
- Diagnostics Needed:
  - MRI Brain/Spine, CT Scan, EEG (Electroencephalogram), EMG (Electromyography). Bring old scan films and reports.
