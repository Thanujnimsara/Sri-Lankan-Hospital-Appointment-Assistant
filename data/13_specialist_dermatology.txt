Specialist Guide: Consultant Dermatologist (Skin Specialist):
- When to See a Dermatologist:
  - Persistent skin rashes, eczema, psoriasis, severe acne, allergic reactions, unexplained skin lesions, mole changes, hair loss (alopecia), or nail disorders.
- Preparing for a Dermatology Appointment:
  - Avoid applying thick makeup, heavy creams, or topical ointments on the affected area on the day of consultation.
  - Bring a list of previous skin creams, soaps, and oral medications tried.
  - Photograph transient skin flare-ups or rashes that come and go so the consultant can view them.
