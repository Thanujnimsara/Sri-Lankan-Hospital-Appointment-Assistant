Specialist Guide: Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon:
- When to See an Orthopedic Specialist:
  - Bone fractures, joint pain (knee, hip, shoulder), ligament injuries, cartilage damage, osteoarthritis, back pain, sciatica, and sports injuries.
- What to Expect & Preparation:
  - Physical mobility examination.
  - Bring existing X-rays, MRI scans, or ultrasound reports.
  - Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy inspection of affected joints (e.g. knees, ankles).
- Treatments Offered:
  - Conservative management (physiotherapy, injections), arthroscopic surgery, joint replacement (knee/hip), and spinal stabilization.
