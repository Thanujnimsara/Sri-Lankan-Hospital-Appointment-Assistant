Guide to Specialist Selection: Consultant Physician (VP) vs General OPD:
- General OPD Medical Officer:
  - Best for initial consultation, mild fever, cough, routine blood test review, minor infections, flu, and general wellness checks.
  - No prior booking required; walk-in available 24/7 at all major hospitals.
- Consultant Physician / Consultant VP (Visiting Physician / General Medicine Specialist):
  - Best for complex medical issues, unexplained prolonged fever, multi-organ symptoms, uncontrolled diabetes/hypertension management, chronic fatigue, or comprehensive health evaluations.
  - Requires booking an appointment via channelling service.
  - Recommended to bring past medical records, lab reports, and current medication lists to the VP appointment.
