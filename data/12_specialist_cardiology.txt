Specialist Guide: Consultant Cardiologist & Cardiac Care:
- When to See a Cardiologist:
  - Chest pain, chest tightness, palpitations, shortness of breath on exertion, unexplained dizziness, high blood pressure, or family history of heart disease.
  - Sub-specialities: Interventional Cardiologist (angiography/stenting), Electrophysiologist (heart rhythm problems), Pediatric Cardiologist.
- Clinic Preparation & Recommended Diagnostics:
  - Bring recent ECG (Electrocardiogram), 2D Echocardiogram report, Lipid profile, and Blood Pressure logs if available.
  - Fasting for 10-12 hours may be required if lipid profile tests are scheduled on the same day.
- Emergency Caution:
  - Acute chest pain radiating to jaw, left arm, or back with sweating is a MEDICAL EMERGENCY. Call Suwa Seriya 1990 immediately.
