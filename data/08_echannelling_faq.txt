eChannelling Booking & Service FAQ:
- What is eChannelling?
  - eChannelling is Sri Lanka's pioneer digital health portal (SLT-Mobitel service) connecting patients with over 250+ private and government hospitals nationwide.
- How to Book via eChannelling:
  1. Visit www.echannelling.com or open the eChannelling App.
  2. Search by Doctor Name, Specialization, or Hospital.
  3. Select available date and appointment number.
  4. Enter Patient Details (Name, NIC, Mobile Number).
  5. Complete Payment (Credit/Debit card, Mobitel bill, eZ Cash).
- Reference Number Verification:
  - Upon successful booking, an 8-digit Reference Code & Appointment Number is sent via SMS.
  - Show this SMS or reference slip at the hospital channelling desk to collect your queue token.
- Customer Support: Dial 225 from any Mobitel line or +94 71 022 5225 from any network.
